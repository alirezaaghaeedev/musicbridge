# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique technical work without harassment, discrimination, threats, or sharing private information. Respect contributors' time and do not pressure anyone to reveal credentials or personal circumstances.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Use a private maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools for sensitive concerns; do not publish another person's private information in a conduct report. No formal response deadline or commercial support is offered.
